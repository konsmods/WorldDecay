# World Decay [B42]

Transforms Knox County into a decaying post-apocalyptic wasteland.

## Features

### Nature & Vegetation
- Trees, bushes, grass reclaiming natural ground and roads
- Vines growing on exterior walls and fences (directional variants)
- Indoor overgrowth - grass and bushes inside buildings
- Ground overlays - dead leaves, debris, trash, cracked tiles
- Seasonal vegetation bias (dry grass in winter, lush in summer)

### Urban Decay
- Burnt/damaged exterior walls
- Barricades on windows and doors (wooden planks, metal sheets)
- Broken doors and windows
- Fence damage - bent (3 severity levels) and destroyed
- Roof destruction on upper floors
- Road and dirt crack overlays

### Vehicle Decay
- Rust and blood stains on abandoned vehicles
- Damaged or missing parts (engine, tires, brakes, windows, etc.)
- Per-category damage chances and condition ranges

### World Age Scaling
- Decay increases as the world gets older
- Amplify mode (full from day one, grows to cap) or Progressive mode (starts near zero, ramps up)
- Growth curves: linear, logarithmic, quadratic
- Per-category speed factors (nature, urban, vehicles)
- Incremental re-decay - explored areas continue decaying over time

### Cleaning System
- Right-click context menu to clean vegetation
- Single tile, 3x3, 5x5, or 10x10 area
- Foraging skill check
- Cleaned tiles are marked and won't re-decay

## Optional Submod

### Malik's A Decade Later
Retextures vanilla tiles with a decayed/weathered appearance. Requires WorldDecay core mod.

## Roadmap / Ideas

### Clothes Retexture & New Outfits
Low-quality, worn-out clothing without relying on vanilla's damage/dirt settings. Think:
- Faded and sun-bleached colors
- Torn and patched garments
- Mud-stained and weathered textures
- Outfits that tell a story (survivor, scavenger, hermit, etc.)

### New Occupations / Classes
Roles that make sense a decade into the apocalypse - not traditional jobs, but survival archetypes:

| Class | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Scavenger** | Bonus to carry weight and find rare items in decayed locations |
| **Hermit** | Starts with a hidden base, better at avoiding hordes, reduced social needs |
| **Mechanic** | Can salvage vehicle parts others can't, faster repair speed |
| **Forager** | Expert at finding food in the overgrown world, herbal medicine knowledge |
| **Sentinels** | Bonus to base defense, can reinforce structures beyond normal limits |
| **Nomad** | No base, moves faster on foot, vehicles last longer, can sleep anywhere |
| **Hunter** | Tracking bonuses, better with firearms, can craft traps |

These would complement the mod's decay theme - survivors who adapted to a world that's been falling apart for years.

3. Optionally enable **WorldDecay_Maniks_a_decade_later** for retextured tiles
4. Back up your saves before enabling or removing
