title=World Decay [B42] [Playtest]
description=Transforms Knox County into a decaying post-apocalyptic wasteland. When each map chunk loads, the mod runs its generators procedurally: vegetation (trees, bushes, grass, vines), structural decay (cracks, burnt walls, broken doors and windows, bent fences), trash and barricades.
description=
description=It keeps a persistent chunk cache to skip already visited areas and a batched dispatcher to avoid lag spikes. It includes cleaning tools and full sandbox control over every element.
description=
description=[h1]Key Features[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*]Vegetation system - trees, bushes, grass and vines with configurable percentages via sandbox
description=[*]Vegetation clustering - trees, bushes and grass objects can form natural groups, with separate enable and boost settings
description=[*]Per-species tree controls, roof vegetation, and real grass objects with depth
description=[*]Urban decay - cracked roads, burnt walls, broken doors and windows, bent and broken fences
description=[*]Construction-safe decay - player-built walls, doors, windows, fences, barricades, and furniture are protected
description=[*]Barricades - leftover barricades from people who held out and lost, scattered across the map
description=[*]Trash and debris - piled-up garbage left behind by years of abandonment
description=[*]Vehicle deterioration - rust, blood stains, damaged/missing parts
description=[*]Roof destruction on upper floors
description=[*]Indoor overgrowth
description=[*]Cleaning system - right-click menu to remove vegetation and clear areas for rebuilding
description=[*]World Age Scaling - decay increases as the world gets older
description=[*]Incremental Re-Decay - already explored areas continue to decay over time
description=[*]Seasonal vegetation - trees grow a seasonal foliage crown, bushes bloom, grass dries in autumn/winter, and vines cycle through growth/dieback frames, updating live as seasons/snow change
description=[*]Seasonal updates work in singleplayer and multiplayer, including already-generated loaded areas
description=[*]Full sandbox customization - every element can be tuned, enabled or disabled
description=[/list]

description=[h1]Performance Notice[/h1]
description=This mod changes world generation heavily. Trees, bushes and grass objects are real world objects with depth and occlusion, so higher densities cost more generation, rendering and save time than flat overlays.
description=
description=First load: Some lag is normal while visible chunks are generated. Processed chunks are cached and the dispatcher becomes mostly idle until new chunks, re-decay, or seasonal updates need work.
description=
description=[b]Performance settings:[/b] Time Budget controls generation work per frame; lower it for smoother FPS. Fast Travel Time Budget does the same while driving. Scan Interval controls how often new chunks are searched; Scan Radius is the area searched, while Priority Radius is the near-player area processed first.
description=
description=[b]Quick FPS tuning:[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Stutter while generating? Lower Time Budget first. Lower = smoother, but chunks finish later.
description=[*]Stutter while driving? Lower Fast Travel Time Budget, then reduce Fast Travel Scan/ Priority Radius.
description=[*]Periodic hitching? Raise Scan Interval or reduce Scan Radius.
description=[*]Still slow when the mod is idle? Lower object density or disable features you do not use, especially trees, bushes, grass, vines and overlays.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Recommendations:[/b] Lower object densities first if you experience lag, especially trees, bushes and grass objects. In multiplayer, host and server specs matter a lot.
description=

description=Place this mod as close to the TOP of your load order as possible. If other mods overwrite the same world tiles, the load order decides which one wins.
