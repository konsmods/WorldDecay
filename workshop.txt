description=Transforms Knox County into a decaying post-apocalyptic wasteland: abandoned streets, nature reclaiming buildings, scattered debris, ruined structures, and deteriorating vehicles.
description=
description=WorldDecay works chunk by chunk. It generates nearby areas in small, prioritized batches, remembers completed chunks, and later revisits them for seasonal changes and optional re-decay. This keeps new exploration responsive while letting the world continue to age.
description=
description=[b]Stability disclaimer:[/b] WorldDecay is now marked Stable, but bugs and game-breaking issues can still happen. Fixes will be addressed as soon as possible, but this is maintained by just one dude with real-life responsibilities as well, so please be patient, report problems clearly, and always back up your saves.

description=[h1]Performance[/h1]
description=The first visit to an area needs generation work; completed chunks are then skipped until re-decay or a seasonal refresh is due. Overlays are flat visual decals and are the lighter option. Trees, bushes, grass objects, and vines are real world objects with depth and occlusion, so they cost more to generate, render, and save. Mix both systems freely, or favor overlays for a lighter setup.
description=
description=[b]Quick FPS tuning:[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Generation stutter: lower Time Budget. Work is spread out more, so chunks finish later.
description=[*]Driving stutter: lower Fast Travel Time Budget first; then reduce Fast Travel Scan Radius or Priority Radius.
description=[*]Regular hitches: raise Scan Interval or reduce Scan Radius.
description=[*]Low FPS even while idle: reduce object densities or disable features you do not use, especially trees, bushes, grass objects, vines, and overlays.
description=[/list]
