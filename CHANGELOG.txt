[h1]2.3.1[/h1]
[list]
[*]Improved multiplayer chunk scheduling with real-time per-player scans, fair queue shares, and startup scans that finish discovery while players stand still.
[*]Added normal and fast-travel scan intervals in seconds, plus clearer scheduler status diagnostics and performance-tuning guidance.
[*]Protected multi-tile fences and doors from unsafe full-removal calls that could cause tile-object errors while streaming.
[/list]

[h1]2.3.0[/h1]
[list]
[*]Reworked chunk discovery into a timer-driven, high/low priority scheduler with resumable work, stale-queue cleanup, queue caps, and fast-travel backpressure.
[*]Added normal and fast-travel scheduler controls. Lower budgets smooth frames but delay generation; shorter intervals refresh sooner; scan radius is coverage and priority radius is the near-player bubble. Both modes use the same order, while fast travel also has its speed threshold.
[*]Added Re-Decay Check Interval (days), which controls how often nearby completed chunks are checked separately from the per-chunk re-decay interval.
[*]Removed LoadChunk generation discovery; periodic scans are now the reliable generation and re-decay backstop. LoadChunk remains for seasonal visual refreshes.
[*]Improved debug tools with Status and Map tabs, a context-menu panel entry, and overwrite/forced/timer-respecting regeneration actions.
[*]Fixed cleanup for WorldDecay special-object vegetation. Player and debug cleaning now remove re-decay vegetation and prevent normal re-decay from restoring cleaned squares.
[*]Removed costly vegetation clustering scans and made scheduler diagnostics debug-mode only.
[/list]
