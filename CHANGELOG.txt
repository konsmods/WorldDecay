[list]

[*]Rebalanced bush, grass overlay, and vine overlay density defaults.
[*]Fixed floor overlays occasionally appearing twice, and fence break/bend chance selection.
[*]Renamed sandbox pages, added per-feature toggles, and moved dividers into the WDecay namespace.
[*]Major performance pass: the mod now goes fully idle once an area is generated, with smarter scan backoff and no more chunks stuck failing indefinitely at the edge of the loaded area.
[/list]

[h1]2.1.0[/h1]
[list]
[*]Vegetation now evolves with real vanilla seasonal sprites and updates live as seasons/snow change.

[/list]

[h1]2.0.2[/h1]
[list]
[*]Fixed fence generator crash spam: isEntityObject() used obj:getClass():getSimpleName(), which Kahlua can't reflect as an indexable table in B42, throwing a RuntimeException on every fence-bearing square. Now uses instanceof(obj, "IsoEntity").
[*]Stopped vines spawning on squares containing construction_* objects (scaffolding/construction objects).
[/list]
