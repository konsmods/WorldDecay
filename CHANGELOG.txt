[h1]2.1.4 - PENDING[/h1]
[list]
[*]Renamed sandbox pages to use the full World Decay name and moved divider options into the WDecay namespace.
[*]Added per-feature toggles (Trees, Bushes, Grass, Vines, Overlays, Barricades, Destroyed Doors & Windows, Walls, Fences) to the main sandbox page so a disabled feature is skipped entirely during generation instead of just being set to 0%.
[*]Major performance pass: the mod now goes fully idle once an area is generated and stays idle, instead of continuing to do per-chunk and per-tick work indefinitely regardless of settings. Chunk-load reseasoning (Seasonal Vegetation Bias) is now properly gated by its own sandbox option -- previously it ran unconditionally on every chunk load even with the setting off. If you had it off and are curious, this is likely the largest single reduction.
[*]Reworked the periodic chunk scan to back off automatically once an area stops producing new work, and to fully unregister its per-tick handler when no feature is enabled at all.
[*]Fixed two silent chunk-processing failure paths that used to just drop a chunk and rely on chance to retry it: a chunk with no grid square available yet, and a chunk unloaded by the game mid-processing before its next tick could resume. Both now retry a bounded number of times, then back off from that specific chunk for a while instead of being re-attempted immediately by every scan and chunk load. Chunks stuck at the edge of the loaded area (a chunk that keeps loading and unloading) could previously fail indefinitely this way, showing up as ongoing Lua-call cost even while standing still, with no log at all until now.
[*]First-time chunk generation keeps the redecay/reseason bookkeeping needed if World Age Redecay or Seasonal Vegetation Bias is enabled later, while feature generators themselves remain fully gated when disabled.
[/list]
